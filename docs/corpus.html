## Full Corpus Explorer

The table below contains all 861 tracks with their Spotify audio features. Use the column filters to search by genre, artist, or feature range.

```{r}
#| echo: false
corpus |> select(Genre,`Track Name`,`Artist Name(s)`,Tempo,Energy,Danceability,Valence,Loudness,Popularity) |>
  mutate(Tempo=round(Tempo,1),Energy=round(Energy,3),Danceability=round(Danceability,3),Valence=round(Valence,3),Loudness=round(Loudness,1)) |>
  datatable(filter="top",options=list(pageLength=15,scrollX=T,dom='frtip',columnDefs=list(list(className='dt-center',targets=3:8))),
    class="table table-striped table-hover table-sm",rownames=F)
```
